Measure it, one trial at a time.

A guided workbench for physical tests, observations and original logs. Nothing starts until you choose Start recording.

Before you begin · what you need
  1. Use an empty box with securely mounted sensors. Keep USB power connected.
  2. Keep the server and ESP32 on networks that can reach each other. Check the device is online below.
  3. For light tests, put the bare LDR inside the box with its resistor divider. Keep the magnet on the lid.
  4. For motion tests, close the box, keep it upright and still. In Arduino Serial Monitor at 115200: send d to disarm, c to capture reference, then a to arm. Check that arming succeeds. This page cannot arm the board.
  5. Have a phone camera for independent evidence. A protractor helps with tilt; temperature/humidity accuracy needs a separate suitable reference instrument.
  6. Start with a pilot. Do not tune thresholds using your final evaluation trials.

No dropping, destructive impact or unsafe heating is required here. Impacts, calibrated environment accuracy and power measurements need a separately defined setup and reference. This workbench does not perform those automatically.
